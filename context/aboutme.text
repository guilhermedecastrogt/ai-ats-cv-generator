# About Me

Paste your professional profile here.

You can paste from any source: your current CV, a bio, a personal summary, a portfolio page, or anything you have written about yourself professionally.

The AI will read this as your main source of professional information.

Include as much as possible:
- Full name, location, email, phone, LinkedIn, GitHub, portfolio
- Work eligibility (visa, citizenship, right to work)
- Professional summary
- Work experience (company, role, dates, responsibilities, stack)
- Education
- Skills and technologies
- Projects
- Certifications and courses
- Languages

The more detail you provide, the stronger and more accurate your CVs will be.
